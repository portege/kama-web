How it works — in four steps

1

Upload your content

Drop in PDFs, text files, or paste URLs. Import prices as CSV and product specs as Excel. BABEH processes everything automatically.

2

AI indexes everything

Documents are chunked, embedded into a vector database, and indexed for full-text search. Prices and specs are stored in a structured database.

3

Embed the widget

Add two lines of code to your website. The chat widget runs in an isolated Shadow DOM — no conflicts with your styles or scripts.

4

Customers get answers

Users ask in natural language. The hybrid search engine finds relevant content, and the LLM generates accurate, cited responses in real time.

Unggah konten Anda
